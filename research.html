Research

Generative AI and the shape of student success.

My work sits at the meeting point of higher education research, institutional data, and the rapid arrival of AI in college learning.

Dissertation

In development

Working focus: how STEM undergraduates use generative AI tools for learning, and how those patterns relate to engagement, persistence, and sense of belonging.

Questions I’m currently refining:

  • How do students’ uses of AI — tutoring, drafting, checking, navigating — differ across gateway STEM courses?
  • Are those uses distributed equitably across first-generation, international, and underrepresented students?
  • How do course policies and instructor framing shape what students feel permitted to try?

This agenda is still taking shape. I welcome conversations with scholars working on related questions.
